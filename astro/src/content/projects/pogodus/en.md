title: "Weather app"
---

Objective: To stand out among candidates for an internship at the Lebedev Studio. The test task requires you to design a paid weather application.

I never use forecasts. Therefore, the first step was research - who needs this anyway?! It turned out to be a lot of people. About 10 different scenarios emerged. I didn’t go into the field of aviation and navigation because they have their own advanced systems.

Among the interests of ordinary people, I have highlighted: travel, orienteering, fishing, home apiary, weather sensitive, yachting and hiking.

So my application meets the needs of ordinary people with unusual interests, which are influenced by many weather factors.

I stood out among the candidates. The project was “hot” and on the cover of the vacancy.

Made in 4 days in 2023
