title: "Pogodus weather app"
---

Brief: stand out among candidates for an internship at Art. Lebedev Studio. The test assignment was to design a paid weather app.

I never use forecasts, so the first step was research: who needs this anyway?! Plenty of people, it turned out — I found about 10 different scenarios. I left out aviation and shipping because they have their own advanced systems.

Among the interests of ordinary people I picked travel, orienteering, fishing, home beekeeping, weather sensitivity, sailing and hiking.

So my app covers the needs of ordinary people with unusual interests that depend on many weather factors.

And I did stand out: the project made it into the “hot” selection and onto the cover of the vacancy.

Made in 4 days in 2023
