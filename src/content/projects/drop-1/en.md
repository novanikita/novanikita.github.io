---

Brief: have fun and make a T-shirt collection for like-minded people.

Whoever wears this T-shirt becomes the first in their own life. Whose opinion do you listen to? Who will you make happy today? Whose feelings matter? Who will you take care of? Everything starts with you. The rest comes later.

It’s a well-made everyday piece with a meaning, so the buyer thinks about themselves, not about some Nikita. That’s why I tested different suppliers of blank T-shirts until I found the perfect oversized one, 300 gsm. Then, at an embroidery workshop, I happened to notice iridescent thread. It turned out that in several years they had never managed to sell gradient embroidery: the thread gives unpredictable results, and every piece comes out different.

Exactly what I needed!

To make each one even more personal, every T-shirt has its own number next to the letter.

Made in 1 week in 2024
