title: "Living identity for a promo campaign"
cardTitle: "Promo campaign for VkusVill"
---

In collaboration with the SenseSay agency

Brief: show how a brand can change with generative identity. This is live-data branding, an approach invented at SenseSay.

VkusVill stores accept waste for recycling. We decided to turn this into a ritual and visualize it, so we came up with a rule: for every kilogram of waste a customer recycles, the company plants a tree, and a generated tree appears in the customer’s account to remember it.

The challenge was to work carefully with VkusVill’s well-established style: we had to invent something new that would fit naturally with what already exists.

The project was shortlisted at the Sreda new design festival in 2024.

Made in 3 months in 2024

Team: Sasha Nikiforov, Artyom Kashekhlebov and me
