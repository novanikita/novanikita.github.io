Brief: propose a new layout for the maps inside metro cars.

The main focus was simplifying the map. I corrected the spelling of station names, chose a condensed typeface because the names are long, added a few major landmarks, showed the route to the airport as an infographic and made the structure of the map more geometric.

Made in 2 months in 2021
