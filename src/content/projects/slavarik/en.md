---
title: "Website \"Slavyarik\""
---

Task: to design a website for a special project of the ENDY agency.

In the spring of 2023, a new law was issued that called for replacing Anglicisms with Russian words. We at the agency decided to show how the law works in practice.

We collected 19 Anglicisms from the digital sphere and offered an alternative for each word from native Russian words. And I designed an interactive website and advertising for social networks.

The UX system appeared thanks to the carpet in the ENDY office. Each card was accompanied by a hover animation so you could get stuck on the site.

Made in 4 weeks in 2023
