title: "Slavyarik website"
---

Brief: design a website for a special project of the ENDY agency.

In spring 2023, a new law called for replacing anglicisms with Russian words. At the agency we decided to show how the law works in practice.

We collected 19 anglicisms from the digital industry and suggested an alternative made of native Russian words for each. I designed the interactive website and the social media ads.

The UX system was inspired by the carpet in the ENDY office. Every card has a hover animation, so you can get stuck on the site for a while.

Made in 4 weeks in 2023
