Task: to design posters for the clinic of the future.

Posters are needed to reveal the value proposition of the Elafix brand. This is a prosthetics clinic from the future, where operations will be replaced with smart tablets, which, based on AI, will determine whether it is necessary to fuse a bone, remove a blood clot, or restore soft tissue.

Made in 2 months in 2020
