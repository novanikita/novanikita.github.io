---
title: "Dodo app promo campaign"
---

Brief: design an ad campaign for the Dodo Pizza app in Turkey and the UAE

What made it hard:

- A different mindset. The ads had to be clear and readable for people with a different mentality, without puns or double meanings.
- Launching in new countries. We had no experience of what works better there, so we wrote 4 headlines, each highlighting a different benefit of the app, to test them.
- A wide range of media. One campaign ran on 9 different media at once, from digital to the pizzeria, from a small table sticker to lightboxes.
- Versatile design. The message had to work in different situations: for cold and warm audiences, in fast zones and slow zones.

Made in 3 months in 2023
