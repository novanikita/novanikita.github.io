Every day I hear designers, managers and executives say that people are stupid, so design and copy have to be made for stupid people.

But who are these “people”? Where do your loved ones, friends and acquaintances end and some “people” begin? And such stupid ones at that.

It’s actually a spell. By saying “well, people are stupid”, a designer gives themselves permission to make complete crap. The spell also takes away the shame for the result, because “people are stuuupid”.

Working by this principle, it’s easier to explain why the design isn’t working. In this system there are smart people, talents, geniuses and cuties, and, amazingly, they have all gathered in one room with you. And then there are some stupid people out there. With a sample like that, it’s clear who’s to blame: whoever can’t grasp the vision of humanity’s great minds. So you have to dumb things down for them.

And what is it really like?

In truth, people have a floating focus and scattered attention. Your work can claim only 1–5% of their attention, and only if they really want to spend it. The rest is their life: work, goals, problems, an itchy butt and family. Add to that clip thinking, ADHD, social media and the emotions from everything happening every day.

That’s how all the stupid people turn out to be ordinary you and me. People who live as best they can. Most likely for the first time.
