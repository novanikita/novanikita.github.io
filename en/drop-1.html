Drop “First letter of the alphabet” No. 1

Goal: have fun and create a collection of T-shirts for like-minded people.

Everyone who wears such a T-shirt will be the first in their life. Whose opinion will you listen to? Who will you make happy today? Whose feelings matter? Who will you care about? It all starts with you. The rest will come later.

This is a quality item with a thought for every day. So that the buyer thinks not about some Nikita, but about himself. So I tested different blank t-shirt suppliers. I have not yet found the perfect oversized one with a density of 300 grams. Then, by chance, at an embroidery production facility, I noticed iridescent threads. It turned out that for several years of work they had never been able to sell gradient embroidery. Because the threads give unpredictable results, the embroideries turn out different.

Just what you need!

To enhance individuality, each T-shirt has a number next to the letter.

Made in 1 week in 2024
