Drop “First letter of the alphabet” No. 2

“The First Letter of the Alphabet” is a collection of things that I make for my friends and like-minded people. Each time a new topic is chosen. For the second drop, I digitized my illustrations with frogs. I usually put a lot of meaning into my work, but this time I wanted to make it simple, cute and meaningless.
