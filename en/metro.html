New metro map of St. Petersburg

Task: To propose a new layout option for placement in carriages.

The main focus was on simplifying the circuit. The spelling of station names has been corrected. A narrow font was chosen because the names are quite long. Several major landmarks have been added. The route to the airport is illustrated with infographics. The structure of the diagram has been made more geometric.

Made in 2 months in 2021
